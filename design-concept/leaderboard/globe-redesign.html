What changes

  1. 1Opens facing the members. Europe, the Middle East and Asia are in view on load. The US (35.2B) turns into view as it spins.
  2. 2Glowing dots, not holes. A pink dot with a soft halo marks a country with members; a tall yellow pin, sized by tokens, marks one with tokens.
  3. 3Every pin is labelled with its flag and name, so you don't have to hover to know where it is.
  4. 4Tap or hover for details, so phones get the tooltip too. Messages over the globe sit on a dark pill so they're readable.
  5. 5No column of dashes. Countries with tokens are ranked; the rest are chips under "Also on the globe".
