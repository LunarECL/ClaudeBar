EVERY STATE OF A CARD

The status colours are the level's own: pipe green, coin gold, fire red, and grey stone when a quota is empty. A quota near its limit says HURRY UP!, as the music does when time runs out.

HOW IT MAPS ONTO CLAUDEBAR'S THEME

Most of it is existing AppThemeProvider properties set to new values; with cardBorderWidth above 1 it is an outlined theme, so the big numbers already print outlined, like Pop's (#499). Two things are new, and their defaults leave every current theme unchanged.

CONCEPT · NOT BUILT YET
